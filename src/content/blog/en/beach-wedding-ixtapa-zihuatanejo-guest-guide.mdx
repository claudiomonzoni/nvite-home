A beach wedding in Ixtapa-Zihuatanejo may bring together guests traveling from different cities or countries. For them, the celebration starts before the ceremony: they need to know which area to visit, where to stay, how to reach the venue, and which activities are included.

Use this guide to prepare the travel details you can share on one invitation or event page. It does not replace directions from your hotel or coordination with your vendors. It gives guests one reliable place to find the information you have confirmed.

> **The most helpful itinerary makes it clear where to be and when.** Optional plans can inspire the trip; confirmed details help the weekend run smoothly.

## 1. Be specific about Ixtapa and Zihuatanejo

Ixtapa and Zihuatanejo are nearby areas within the same region, but the destination name alone may not guide a first-time visitor. Use the full name of your hotel, beach, garden, or venue and include a map link that points to the correct entrance.

If the ceremony and reception are in different places, share both locations and explain how guests will travel between them. Do not assume everyone knows the distances or local transportation options.

## 2. Explain how to arrive without booking every guest’s trip

The area is served by Zihuatanejo International Airport, with the airport code **ZIH**. Guests can check current flight information directly with their airline and consult [OMA’s official Zihuatanejo airport information](https://www.oma.aero/en/oma-group/contact/airport-directory.htm).

Next, explain any group transportation you have arranged and where it departs. If each guest needs to arrange their own ride, say so early and share a hotel or transportation contact only when it is confirmed. Avoid publishing prices, schedules, or availability you have not checked recently.

## 3. Recommend lodging with concrete booking details

If you have a host hotel, room block, or group rate, share:

- the hotel name and address;
- how to book and when the rate expires;
- a group code, if there is one;
- what to do if the room block sells out;
- the route or approximate distance to the wedding venue, once verified.

If you are offering several options, organize them by proximity or type of stay. Tell guests that they should check availability and terms directly with each property. For visitors unfamiliar with the area, knowing which part of town to focus on is more useful than receiving a long list without context.

## 4. Separate the ceremony, reception, and optional plans

Create a schedule that lists the date, time, location, and dress code for every confirmed activity. If you are hosting a welcome dinner or next-day gathering, label whether it is part of the invitation or optional.

For an outdoor celebration, share practical venue notes: access over sand, uneven paths, stairs, available shade, or footwear suggestions. Confirm these details with your venue because conditions vary from one beach and season to another.

## 5. Help guests prepare for the beach

Describe the dress code in words people can understand: formal garden attire, tropical formal, or comfortable clothing for a seaside gathering. If the event is directly on the sand or the venue has a special weather recommendation, confirm it with your vendors before sharing it.

You may also suggest things to do during free time. [Visit Mexico’s Ixtapa-Zihuatanejo guide](https://visitmexico.com/en/recomendacion/32/10-imperdibles-de-ixtapa-zihuatanejo) highlights places and activities around the area. Include only ideas you know or have checked, and clearly separate optional sightseeing from wedding events.

## 6. Collect RSVPs with the details that affect logistics

Set an RSVP deadline that gives you enough time to confirm counts with your venue and caterer. If you are arranging group transportation or need meal information, ask for those details in the response process.

Make clear how many seats each invitation includes and how guests can update their reply. For more help with reminders and response tracking, read our guide to [organizing beach wedding RSVPs](/en/blog/organize-beach-wedding-rsvp-guests).

## Final review before sharing your invitation

Open the event page on a phone and check that:

1. The map links open at the correct venue entrance.
2. Times include the date and local time zone.
3. Hotel rates and booking deadlines are still current.
4. Transportation details include a confirmed pickup point and contact.
5. The schedule distinguishes confirmed events from optional plans.
6. The RSVP shows its deadline, assigned seats, and any questions you need answered.

## Beach wedding in Ixtapa-Zihuatanejo FAQs

### Which airport should guests use for Ixtapa-Zihuatanejo?

The region is served by Zihuatanejo International Airport, whose IATA code is ZIH. Guests should confirm flight details directly with the airline and airport before traveling.

### What lodging information should I send?

Share the host hotel, nearby options, booking instructions, group-rate deadline, and confirmed contact details. Explain whether each guest is responsible for making their own reservation.

### What should guests know about a beach wedding?

Along with the time and address, explain whether the event is on sand, what dress code to expect, and whether the venue has specific access instructions. Confirm these details with the venue.

### How should I organize guest transportation?

If you arrange group transportation, share the pickup location, schedule, capacity, and how guests can reserve seats. If you do not arrange it, say so clearly and share only options you have verified.
